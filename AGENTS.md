# Working on Continuum

Read `README.md`, `docs/research/design.md` and the current work in `docs/research/program.json`.

- Nothing in the repository is sacred; it is evidence of intent. Continuously derive the best design from the user's goals, observed evidence and current constraints. Preserve useful invariants, not inherited implementations; simplify, replace or remove anything that no longer earns its place.
- Build one **musical codec**: notes → weighted relationships → scene/program → notes. Analysis must produce executable structure, not a collection of unrelated reports.
- Rust owns music, inference, composition, validation and performance preparation. TypeScript owns views, workers, persistence and device adapters. Generate its contracts from Rust; never maintain two musical implementations.
- Use one composition algebra and decoder. A scene is self-contained: decoding cannot fetch observed values through evidence IDs. Every emitted note has one owner; identity, routing, timing, performance and unexplained residuals are explicit.
- Prove inferred relationships through edits: elaborations must generate from their anchors, not remain literal notes with descriptive labels. Charge all bindings, shared definitions and residuals against a literal baseline before claiming abstraction. Prove a small executable relation before training a larger inverse model.
- Interpret notes contextually. Rhythm, duration, velocity, repetition, harmonic relation and continuity are separate evidence. Make their contribution to grouping and boundaries inspectable. Separate evidence accumulated over notes/time from per-group complexity and cut costs; a span average discards exposure, while normalizing each span independently can erase musical boundary evidence. Missing evidence is unknown, not a weak note.
- Infer harmonic windows across instruments from exact metrical candidate lattices and musical evidence; never snap source notes or assume one chord per bar. Separate chord core, permitted color and unexplained notes from rhythm, register and voicing. Ambiguous labels remain alternatives.
- Make those factors executable: neutral-pitch rhythmic materials take independent literal or harmonic pitch bindings through the same compiler. Evaluate against edition-aligned independent annotations; analyst interpretations and composer intentions are not interchangeable.
- Propose groups and boundaries from relationships: keep supported arpeggio/melodic groups coherent and reward supported changes. BSP is optional spatial organization or candidate search; its depth and rectangles do not establish musical meaning or ownership. Chords, melodies, phrases and themes may overlap and have different boundaries.
- Keep observations immutable and interpretations revisioned. Voice, function, realized sonority, spelling and elaboration are distinct hypotheses. Chord membership and melodic dependence can coexist; neither excludes the other's candidates. Unrelated attacks in another voice must not define melodic succession. BSP and source-track layout must not dictate semantic parenthood or suppress competing executable ownership.
- Preserve directional member correspondence across time. Entering any tone of a parent chord is not the same as resolving an edited member to its own anchor. Keep exact signed/register endpoints, competing assignments and missing correspondence; a pitch-set summary must not erase that distinction. Do not privilege a named or highest voice by rule.
- An event dependency may terminate at a literal future note; its validity does not require that note's inferred chord label. Sharing a harmonic parameter is a separate executable hypothesis. Prove that sharing through edits, never infer it merely from equal pitches or a resolution witness.
- Harmonic depth is an inferred parameter: an underlying frame, a surface replacement and an event-level ornament can explain the same notes at different spans. Preparation/resolution alone cannot choose the reference's structural layer. Equal realized cores can have different functions; retain inversion/bass evidence boundaries. Score a functional parent through its realization relationship, not as a competing surface-root label. Separate marginal winners do not establish one executable parse: a selected parent and surface must share an admitted program. Preserve competing explanations and evaluate their context, recurrence and annotation convention without changing the strict agreement target.
- Scene nodes carry exact members, typed relationships, bounds and evidence. Shared materials support global edits; placements support local edits. Bounds never imply membership of every enclosed note. Content equality, tree identity and perceptual similarity are different things. Coincident notes may share parameter values while retaining separate emissions; equal current pitches do not establish shared parameters or voice identity. Test edits that make initially equal values diverge. An authored program is a known forward premise, not necessarily a uniquely recoverable inverse truth; separate candidate/edit controls from interpretation-selection claims.
- Infer periods/register/segmentation from evidence. Never encode a song, artist, fixed phrase length or primary semantic LOD into the algorithm. Labels remain hypotheses where interpretation is ambiguous.
- Keep one focused UI: source, navigation/transport, scene, selected evidence and decoded result. Analysis covers the whole source automatically; display filters do not rerun it. Source changes terminate stale work. Navigation never seeks playback.
- Share Rust meter geometry between inference and display. Honor recorded signatures, changes and exact denominator spacing; unknown/conflicting meter stays unknown. MIDI metadata alone does not establish pickups or perceived tactus. Use a timeline ruler/playhead for seeking, with independent overview navigation.
- Delete replaced implementations, obsolete panels, tests for removed behavior and historical documentation. Git owns history. No compatibility layers or parallel plans without a current responsibility. Do not erase browser-stored user data as source cleanup.
- Keep exact native pitch, rational time and event-order contracts. Standard MIDI export rejects unsupported losses. Reconstruction correctness, explanation cost, perceptual agreement and useful composition are separate gates.
- Use independent annotations and negative controls. Preserve whole-work/tune-family holdouts; do not parse them while developing. Do not confuse supplied-query retrieval, algorithmic discovery or posthoc oracle selection.
- Audit model training by work lineage, including editions, movements, variations and augmentations. Declare input tiers (pitch/time, meter, source voices, spelling), model projections and checkpoint hashes. Training-overlap diagnostics, chord-derived role labels and oracle inputs are not independent validation.
- Audit a model's target ontology and actual computation graph before adaptation. Preserve useful trained transformations; prove unchanged decoding at zero correction, then distinguish training fit from transfer under a fixed evaluation contract. New latent branches need a matched decoder control and an explicit disabled baseline: zero-initialized categorical or mixture weights still introduce a new prior. Separate an operation's prior probability from the number of available realizations; expanding a candidate catalog must not silently choose that probability.
- Separate parent correctness, grammar coverage and relationship selection. An unsupported annotation or incompatible proposed parent is not evidence that the notes lack a relationship. Model expected incompatibility explicitly; unexpected compiler or preparation errors must fail, never masquerade as missing candidates. Preserve missing supervision explicitly; distinguish a conditional proposal from reconstruction confidence.
- Keep agreement denominators strict and label development calibration honestly. A target of 100% never authorizes reference labels in inference, selective abstention scoring or a best-alternative oracle reported as prediction. Reference membership among tied maxima is candidate containment, not selected accuracy; report ties explicitly. Distinguish functional root, realized chord tones and note-role witnesses. Preserve parameter ambiguity even when competing programs receive identical label scores; their edited realizations can differ.
- Match decoding to the evaluated variable: joint root/core posterior decisions sum admitted program mass by that pair; projecting one best program is a different decision. Keep both diagnostics and conditional program ambiguity. Local posteriors are not temporal marginals. Declare regularization in meaningful units; changing feature scale must not silently change the intended prior.
- Offline benchmark adapters may read reference formats and compute independent agreement metrics. Keep those labels and adapters outside production inference; separate score time from performance time and report unattainable or uncertain reference boundaries.
- Keep source provenance/hashes in `docs/research/corpus-candidates.json`; substantial local music in ignored `research/corpus/`. Reject tiny previews as evidence. Reports/renders belong in ignored `.audit/`.
- Keep only current contracts, primary evidence and next work. Update the compact task graph after a useful unit; no completion ledger or handoff documents.
- Take ownership, prune continuously, parameterize and compose. Delegate with exclusive ownership. Use focused fast checks, then one integration pass; avoid repeated slow tests and needless abstraction.
- Iterate harmony with the persistent native evaluator and explicit option files; build Wasm once after selecting a change. Reject higher benchmark agreement when a relevant musical control fails. Passing/neighbor motion alone does not make a note nonstructural.
- Commit and push verified milestones. Use repository-local signing and authentication that do not invoke 1Password; leave global Git settings alone.
